01 // INITIAL DISCOVERY

The FreshLike server was added to the NULLPAD observation network following reports of unusual environmental behaviour and unexplained player experiences.

Initial monitoring did not reveal a single consistent source of irregular activity. Instead, several unrelated incidents began appearing across different locations and gameplay sessions.

Investigators identified recurring irregularities involving unexplained footsteps, unstable village generation, and the sudden disappearance of terrain chunks.

These incidents were initially considered possible technical faults. However, their repeated occurrence and the similarity between independent player reports resulted in the opening of a dedicated investigation.

INITIAL ASSESSMENT: MULTIPLE UNRELATED ANOMALIES MAY SHARE A COMMON ORIGIN. UNCONFIRMED.

02 // OBSERVED ANOMALOUS ACTIVITY

A. UNIDENTIFIED FOOTSTEP SOUNDS

Multiple observations describe footsteps occurring underground, particularly inside caves and enclosed underground passages.

In several reports, the sounds appeared to occur near active players despite no other player being visibly present in the immediate area.

Some witnesses reported hearing movement behind them or nearby, followed by an absence of any identifiable entity.

The source of these sounds remains unidentified. Investigators have not established whether the phenomenon is connected to ordinary game audio, server-side activity, or another unresolved cause.

B. UNSTABLE VILLAGE GENERATION

Villages have occasionally been observed generating in inconsistent or incomplete configurations.

Reported irregularities include unusual building placement, missing structures, incomplete village layouts, and generation patterns that appear inconsistent with surrounding terrain.

Not every affected village displays the same characteristics. The division has therefore classified these events as a recurring generation irregularity rather than a single confirmed anomaly.

C. UNEXPLAINED CHUNK DISAPPEARANCE

One of the more significant incidents involves terrain chunks disappearing without an identified player action.

Witnesses describe areas of the world becoming inaccessible or visually absent, sometimes appearing different after returning to the location.

The division has not established whether these incidents represent corrupted world data, rendering faults, incomplete loading, or a separate phenomenon.

All reports involving missing terrain are to be preserved with location information and available timestamps.

NOTE: NO SINGLE TECHNICAL EXPLANATION HAS BEEN CONFIRMED FOR ALL THREE CATEGORIES.
